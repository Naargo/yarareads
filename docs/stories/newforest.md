# Newforest (series brief)

Tom's new series, Oct 26 2026, worked out with Claude. Julia and Toby books 1-7 become the origin story;
this series starts a year later, with Julia at 7. It's meant to run as long as Beast Quest or Dragon Masters.
Why: Yara wanted shapeshifting ("levitation with feelings isn't cool enough"), so Tom gave her a whole world.

"Newforest" is a working name for the town; see Names.

## The world

- **Newforest** is a small valley town hidden in a remote mountain range (Rockies-like), home to many species and
  things of ancient origin. It's ringed by the **last surviving dragon heartgems**.
- **Heartgems** are pure True Magic, and they give dragons their power. A heartgem **chooses** dragons one at a time.
  Dragons are somehow born from heartgems *and* parents (to be worked out).
- **The Collapse:** long ago, the heartgems died **all at once**, apart from the ones around Newforest.
- **Tribes:** as many as the series needs, and they come back (tribes are reused, not one-and-done). Each one looks
  different, acts different and has a different power: levitation (Toby's), shapeshifting, fire, earth (20-ton tails)...
  **Every tribe has its own colour, and no colour repeats.**
- **True Magic** is emotional magic. It shows as **rainbow sparkles**. It's a trait, not a tribe: a few dragons in any
  tribe carry a little of it, and that sets them apart from normal dragons. **Rainbow is thought of as bad**, so most
  dragons who have it hide it. A True Magic dragon's power follows its feelings, so its tribe sees it as broken.
- **Dragons age slowly** (roughly ten human years to one dragon year). Toby is about 70 and a kid.
- **The townsfolk:** most don't know about dragons at all. A rare few have books. Some think it's an "interesting" story.

## Toby's magic (Tom's system, replacing the old brief's)

His levitation follows what he feels. It's never "solved": over the series he learns to use it.

| Feeling | What floats does |
|---|---|
| Sad | floats low and doesn't move |
| Happy | bounces up and down |
| Mad | flies up really high, then slams down |
| Scared | shoots up high and away, fast, but the fall is cushioned |
| Calm | one thing, held perfectly still, and he doesn't notice (he's thinking; the chair is two feet up) |

Calm is also how he floated Julia in the origin books. Calm is his shield against the grey dragon (see below).

## The villain: the grey dragon (name to come)

- **Looks:** grey and slightly see-through. He's fading. Draw him grand and misty, not a spooky ghost.
- **Was:** rainbow, the colour of True Magic, which he had a ton of. An emotion dragon: once great, now grumpy. Ancient.
- **What happened:** his heartgem collapsed. He holds a grudge against the other dragons, who he believes destroyed it.
- **Magic:** he feels other dragons' emotions and **stokes** them. He can't make a feeling from nothing, but he can find a
  spark of annoyance and blow on it until it's rage. A calm dragon gives him nothing to grab.
- **Cunning:** being half there, he can barely touch the world, so he works through others: he sparks feuds and wars
  between tribes and doesn't fight them himself. Each feud is a trick (a framed theft, a disguise, a whisper), and
  each one is a mystery for Julia to unravel, not a battle.
- **Wants:** to become solid again and to bring back his fallen tribe. Near a heartgem, or when he wins, he gets a
  little more solid and a flicker of rainbow comes back.
- **He can feel every True Magic dragon** (that's how he felt Toby when Julia floated), so he's after the same
  sparkly misfits Julia befriends.
- Julia is his natural enemy: she's the one who's good at feelings, and she can tell when a tribe's anger isn't really theirs.

## The people

- **Julia (7):** intellectual and emotional, the smart one. She figured out Toby's feelings magic.
- **Her best friend:** a boy, a year or two older (8-9), her neighbour. Physical, bold, inspiring: climbs first, thinks
  later. He finds out about dragons in book 1, when Toby gets scared and he suddenly ends up at the height of the treetops.
- **A classmate who's secretly a young shapeshifter dragon** (with True Magic).
- **Mom and Dad both know.** Grandma grew up in Newforest, like Julia; Mom moved away and moved back when Grandma got older.
- **Grandma** isn't one of the main cast (too old for adventures), but now and then she steps in, and she's powerful:
  a dragon mage. There's a **book**: Grandma's old dragon book.

## The shape of each book

- **Each book:** a dragon from a tribe, usually a True Magic misfit whose power swings with its feelings, needs help,
  and Julia has to work out its rules. The grey dragon is after the same dragon.
- **Each arc (4-6 books):** one tribe, rotating kinds of book (mystery, rescue, journey, silly, feud), and the grey
  dragon pushing in at the end.
- **Every book ends with Julia presenting to Mom and Dad** what she found: a page of her own Dragon Book (her drawing
  of the tribe, its power, what she figured out). Keep it fresh: Toby gives a demo and it goes wrong, the friend acts it
  out, the new dragon turns up, the parents ask so much it becomes a row, and once Mom presents something back. What Julia
  leaves out (the dangerous bits) is its own tension. A parent's question at the end can seed the next book. Break
  the ritual on purpose at a big moment (no presentation, because it went too wrong to tell).

## Arc 1: The Shapeshifters (outline draft, for Tom)

A year after Julia floated. The grey dragon has been searching since he woke.

1. **Up in the Trees.** A new boy next door, Dash, sees a sparkle in the woods and goes after it straight over the
   fence. Toby gets a fright and Dash shoots up to the treetops, then drifts down soft. "Again!" Julia has to decide
   whether to trust him. Somewhere, a fox with a rainbow glint in its fur is watching. *Presentation:* Julia presents
   Dash ("He knows now"). Dad asks: "And the fox?"
2. **The New Kid.** Kit, new at class: hungry all the time, gone every recess. Julia notices a rainbow glint in his
   hair, the same one the fox had. Dash wants to chase the fox; Julia wants to wait and watch. The reveal: Kit is a
   young shapeshifter dragon, and his shapes follow his feelings (scared: a fox; mad: something huge). He's hiding
   among humans because rainbow is bad in his tribe. *Presentation:* Kit himself, nervous, as a fox on the rug.
3. **Rainbow Is Bad.** Why would sparkles be bad? Grandma's old dragon book has an answer, and missing pages. Kit's
   tribe is looking for him, and they're angry: someone told them a floating dragon is after their heartgem. Kit is
   scared of going home.
4. **Who Is Who?** A "Toby" is breaking things near the shapeshifters' heartgem, and the tribe blames Toby's tribe from
   Super-Tall Peak. A feud starts. Julia solves it with the magic system: the real Toby's things float when he feels,
   so make "Toby" laugh. Nothing bounces. It's a shapeshifter whose grudge the grey dragon stoked.
5. **The Shapeshifters' Heartgem.** Finale at the gem. The grey dragon (see-through, grey) stokes the elders' fear of
   rainbow until the cave shakes. Toby stays calm, and calm gives the grey dragon nothing to grab. The heartgem glows at
   Kit's sparkles, a hint that rainbow isn't bad at all. The grey dragon gets away a little more solid, a flicker of
   colour in his scales. Kit stays in town, for now.
