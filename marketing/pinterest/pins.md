## 01-tap-a-word

**Title:** A free chapter book with sound buttons on every word

**Description:** Yara Reads is a free chapter book app for kids who just learned their phonics sounds. Every word can be sounded out, and when they get stuck they tap the word to see its sound buttons, with a dot for one letter and a bar for letter pairs like oo and er, just like in class. Works on any tablet. No ads, no account.

**Link:** https://yarareads.finereli.com/about
