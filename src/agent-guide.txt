YARA READS: A GUIDE FOR AGENTS ADDING BOOKS

Yara Reads is a reading app for children of about 5 to 7 who have just learned their phonics sounds. A child taps any
word and its sound buttons appear. You are adding books to one parent's private account. Only that account sees them.

THE ONE RULE
Every word in a story must be readable by the child: either it can be sounded out with the sounds the account has
switched on, or it is one of the account's "tricky words" (read on sight), or it is a character name you list in `names`.
The app checks by pronunciation, not just spelling ("cake" fails if the child doesn't know the split vowel a_e).
Never ask the parent to loosen their sounds to make a story pass. Rewrite the story.

WORKFLOW
1. get_sounds: see which sounds, tricky words and names this account has. Write only with those.
2. Draft the story as short pages (see STYLE). Then check_text on all pages at once. It lists every unreadable word by page.
   Rewrite those words (a simpler word, or a different sentence) and check again. Repeat until nothing is unreadable.
3. save_book with the pages. For kind "story" it refuses text with unreadable words and tells you which.
   Saving again with the same title (or the id you were given) replaces the text and keeps existing pictures on pages that still exist.
4. Pictures (optional but they make the book): set_image for the cover (page 0) and each page, from a URL or base64.
   Use JPEG or PNG or WebP, about 1000 pixels wide, under 3 MB. A web JPEG of 60 to 150 KB is ideal. Keep the characters
   looking the same on every page: describe them the same way, or use one reference image.
5. preview_url returns a link that opens the app read-only as the account for an hour. Open it in a browser tool if you
   have one, page through the book, and check the pictures, the text size and that every word has its sound buttons.
   Fix what you see and preview again.

STYLE
- Pages are short: one to three sentences, under about 25 words. The child reads each page aloud on their own.
- Use the present tense. "-ed" endings are usually not on a beginner's sound list; check_text will say.
- Names go in `names` (for example ["Julia", "Toby"]) and then count as readable anywhere in that book.
- A story with a clear want, a problem and a small win beats a list of facts. Make it something the child would choose.
- Ask the parent what the child likes before writing. Use that.
- A "series" is just a heading in the library: give related books the same series and an increasing number.

TWO KINDS OF BOOK
- kind "story" (default): written for this child, so every word must be readable. save_book enforces it.
- kind "book": a real published book the family owns, typed in as pages. Its words are not checked; the app marks the words the
  child can't sound out yet with a dotted underline, and the parent sees what share is readable. Only add books the parent says they own.
  The book stays private to their account.

LIMITS
At most 40 books per account, 60 pages per book, 600 characters per page. Images up to 3 MB.
